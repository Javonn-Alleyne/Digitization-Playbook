# The R.O.A.D. Initiative Inc. Digitisation Manual 
These series of document provides a comprehensive overview of the digitization workflow present at R.O.A.D. Initiative Inc. .It details the essential steps involved in capturing and processing the images of historic and archic physical objects to create high-fidellity digital representations.

## [Quick Start Guide]

## User Guidue
**[Preliminary Setup](Prelim_Setup/prelim_setup.md)**   
Outlines the essential steps the Digitization Opeartors must follow each morning to prepare their workspace. Ensuring an environment that is favorable for efficient and accurate digitization. 

**[PreFlight](Pre_Flight/pre_flight.md)**   
Outlines the steps to configure the camera settins, also known as calibrating, before capture begins. It aims to achieve consistent baseline for every image taken. Settings include: color reproduction, focus, and optimal lighting conditions.

**[Digitizing Process](Digi_Process/digi_proc.md)** 
Outlines the steps involved in capturing images of the physical object. It involves careful positioning of the object, systematic capturing of each page, cropping and processing of the images to prepare them for further use. This section emphasizes the importance of attention to detail in capturing and processing images to ensure the highest quality.

**[Export](Export/export.md)**  
Outlines the steps for exporting and renaming the fresh digital images taken. It explains the importance of  consistent naming conventions, selection of high-quality export formats, and organized storage to allow for easy retrieval of the digitized images.

**[Shutdown](Shutdown/shutdown.md)**    
Outlines how to properly shutdown the workstations

**[Troubleshoot](Troubleshoot/troubleshoot.md)**    
Outlines the various issues which may arise during any part of the process and providing a solution, if available.
